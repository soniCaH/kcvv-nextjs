Naming what colour and position are saying

Three homepage surfaces encode a distinction with no word attached. On Eerste ploegen the left card is a result and the right card is the next fixture — nothing says so. On the mobile matchstrip the same pair stacks vertically, and the UITSLAG / VOLGENDE words were dropped in #2388 to buy the opponent name 72px. On the news grid a green card means "transfer" and carries no label.

Each option below is applied to all three surfaces, because the acceptance criteria ask for one pattern across all of them. Pick one.

B · Let the outcome word carry it

The result side says Winst / Gelijk / Verlies beside the score, the upcoming side says Volgende. One word does two jobs: it names the kind and it stops the win/loss tint from being colour-only. This is the "outcome word" eerste-ploegen-locked.md specified and the #2301 unification dropped.

Cost: Eerste ploegen ±0px · strip ±0px · news card same as A. Risk: needs a fallback for a played match PSD hasn't scored yet (no outcome word exists), and the word competes with the scoreline for the fixed centre column that #2397 fought over.

C · Head the group with a label

A MonoLabel above each group rather than inside each row — column heads on Eerste ploegen, a heading line on the strip, a pill on the news card. This is the pattern <TeamMatchesSection> already uses on team pages ("Eerstvolgende" above the featured row), and what #2388's own note proposed for the strip.

Cost: Eerste ploegen +18px once per band · strip +22px once · news card +20px per card. Risk: Eerste ploegen's grid collapses to one column below xl, where column heads stop mapping to columns and each card needs its own label anyway — so this option silently becomes option A on mobile.

Locked 2026-08-12 — A, with B's word folded into it. Owner: @climacon.

As shipped: <TeamAgendaRow showKind> opens the caption with Winst / Gelijkspel / Verlies on a settled match, Uitslag on one PSD still owes a score for, Volgende on a scheduled one, and nothing at all on a postponed or cancelled one, where the PP / AFG marker already speaks. The strip's ledger rows carry Uitslag / Volgende under the date; the news card's meta row carries Transfer.

A is the only option that labels every row, survives the mobile stack, and costs nothing on two of the three surfaces — and on the news card it is simply restoring what the lock already said.

B's outcome word is the one thing A lacks: without it the win/loss tint stays colour-only. It fits inside A's caption at no extra height — Winst · 3e Prov. A on the result side, Volgende · 3e Prov. A on the fixture side. That reads as one pattern, satisfies both bullets of the issue, and needs no fallback: when a played match has no score, the caption falls back to Uitslag.
