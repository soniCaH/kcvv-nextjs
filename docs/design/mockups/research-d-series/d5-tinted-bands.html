A fifth band ground — coloured copy paper?

Today a section stands on one of four grounds: cream, cream-soft, ink, jersey-deep. §4.1 proposes a fifth — a saturated-pastel stock with plain ink type on it, so a page gets chapters without spending green.

It names our three aged status tints as the candidates. That is where the problem starts.

Those three tints are already spoken for

--color-alert-soft, --color-warning-soft and --color-success-soft are semantic. The comment above them in globals.css says so outright: they exist to give "the three Alert variants visually-balanced soft bodies", introduced for the Phase 2.A.5 Alert ticket-stub.

So a section band in success-soft is the same colour as "this went well", and one in alert-soft is the same colour as "something is wrong". Below: an actual Alert sitting directly above a section band on the same tint.

The prior question is whether cream-soft already does this job. It is the documented step-down ground, and in option A it is doing exactly what T6 asks for — separating the calendar from the news — just quietly. T6 is really a claim that the step-down is too quiet to chapter a long page. That is a legitimate claim on the homepage and a weak one on a detail page, so if a tint ships, its rule is probably "index pages only, once", not "any page, once".
