#2508 · Q1 · one page, five speeds

How fast should this site move?

The site uses five speeds today and writes none of them down. Change the speed at the top. Then hover the nav, hover a card, press a chip, and replay the timeline. Judge how it feels, not what the number says.

Round 2 fix. The first version of this page locked the curve to cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) — the Q2 pick. Every speed felt the same, and that was the page's fault: that curve puts about 90% of the travel in the first quarter of the time, so on a 4px press-down the rest is invisible. Two things are new:

  • A curve switch, top right. Try linear first — speed becomes obvious. Then switch back to quint and watch it disappear again.
  • A drawer in the strip below (the Menu ▸ button). It travels 320px, not 4px. Duration is unmissable there.

If speed only reads on the drawer and never on a hover, that is not a broken test — that is the answer.

What to look for — in this order

  1. Set curve to linear. Open the drawer at 150ms, then at 500ms. That is the full range this site could ever use.
  2. Stay on linear. Now hover a card at 150ms and at 500ms. Can you feel it on a 4px move?
  3. Switch back to quint. Repeat both. How much of the difference survives the curve?
  4. Last: can you tell 240 from 300 anywhere at all? If not, 240 does not deserve a name.
