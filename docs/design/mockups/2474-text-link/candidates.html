Two highlighter links, one of which has never shipped

.prose-link (globals.css:707) renders on 10 sites. <EditorialLink variant="inline"> renders on zero — its only consumer, <SectionHeader>, passes variant="cta". They are the same idea built twice. This page shows what actually differs.

…or hover any link. The button drives both at once, since you can only hover one.

Test 1 — the decisive one

What happens when a link breaks across two lines

Body copy wraps. A link inside a sentence will sooner or later straddle a line break — and a Sanity editor can make that happen at any time by typing. Both columns below hold the same long link in a narrow measure.

Wraps correctly. box-decoration-break: clone restores the marker on every fragment, and the paired padding-inline: 4px / margin-inline: -4px gives each fragment its 4px overshoot back without shifting the surrounding copy. This was added deliberately — globals.css calls it “the CMS-1 wrapped-link bug”.

Never shipped

<EditorialLink variant="inline">

measure forced to 290px

Lees meer over de praktische informatie voor nieuwe leden voor je inschrijft.

Cannot flow with the sentence. The text does wrap inside the link — but inline-flex makes the whole link an atomic box, so it cannot share a line with the copy around it. Measured: .prose-link returns 2 client rects (it broke across the line like text), EditorialLink returns 1 — one box, pushed onto its own line, splitting the sentence into three chunks. And because the sweep is absolutely positioned to the box, a wrapped link gets one slab under the last line only.

Test 2 — the look

Resting colour, sweep weight, and how the reveal moves

Both reveal left-to-right, but by different mechanisms — .prose-link grows background-size (the slab is cropped open, so its hand-pulled top edge stays put), while EditorialLink scales transform: scaleX (the whole slab is squashed and stretches out, so the asymmetry distorts as it plays). Hit the button in the header to run both. Watch where the slab lands — that turns out to matter more than either the colour or the timing.

What actually differs

The path fork is justified, not drift. globals.css:719 explains it: the 0/100 edges exist because the reveal animates background-position-x: 0, so the slab’s start stays pinned ~4px before the word instead of creeping right. STROKE_PATH’s 1/99 is correct for a scaleX reveal. Neither is wrong — they follow from their mechanism, which is why “just call the component” was never free.

The one thing EditorialLink has that .prose-link lacks: a motion-reduce gate. .prose-link animates regardless of prefers-reduced-motion. That is worth carrying across whichever way this goes.

Test 4 — your remark

Consistency here does not flatten the site

The rule under discussion is two registers, not one. A link inside a sentence and a link that is its own object stay visibly different things — and the affordance register is where a section can still carry its own character. What the rule removes is the accidental variation: four type sizes and three colours applied to the same job by different hands on different days.

unchanged — an action, not a destination

A costs you the two things that carry meaning. The citation row becomes three CTAs stacked in a wrapped list — each shouting at the same volume as a section footer — and the mail/maps links lose their icons, which are the only thing saying “this opens your mail app” before you click.

B costs you a clean rule. “Two registers” is really “cta, plus two exceptions I argued for” — and every exception is where drift starts again. If you want one sentence someone can apply without asking, A is the one that survives contact with the next developer.

Tuning the marker for uppercase

Why the last one looked like nothing: 0.42em anchored at background-position: 0 100% is measured from the bottom of the em box. Lowercase body copy has descenders below the baseline, so the band cuts up through the x-height and reads as a marker. Uppercase has no descenders and taller letterforms, so the identical band sits in the empty gap under the caps and clips only their bottom edge. Same value, different type, different result — it needs its own number. All shown in hover state.
