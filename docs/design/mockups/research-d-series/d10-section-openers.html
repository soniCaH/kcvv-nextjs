S6 — one heavy frameA single very thick border, once per page

Against 2px structure everywhere else, one object per page gets a frame you cannot miss. Fonts In Use runs 75px; 14px is enough at our scale.

Reads as a mounted print. Note it drops the offset shadow — a 14px border and a 6px shadow fight each other, so this is a swap, not an addition. "Once per page" is the whole rule: two is a pattern, three is noise.

Two of the three are gated. Y3 depends on a working overflow: hidden to clip rather than overflow, and #2598's root cause is not yet isolated — so it must not be prototyped against the live homepage until it is. S2 must land on SectionHeader and never on EditorialHeading. S6 is ungated and is a one-line variant on whichever figure gets it.
