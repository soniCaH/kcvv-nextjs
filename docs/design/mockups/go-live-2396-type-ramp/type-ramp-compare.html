The ramp isn’t unenforced. It’s inert — and there are four of them.

Audit of apps/web/src, stories excluded: 273 arbitrary font-size literals across 78 files. The ticket said 31. But the literals are a symptom. Below: what the four systems are, why the bespoke one lost, what repairing it looks like, and how small a label actually needs to be.

1 · Four systems for one job

Two of these are token ramps someone designed, one is Tailwind’s factory default that nobody chose, and one is 273 hand-typed pixels. A developer picking a size has four places to look and no rule saying which.

2 · Q1 — what “repair the ramp” means

globals.css writes --text-label--lh and --text-label--tracking. Tailwind v4 reads --line-height and --letter-spacing. I compiled a probe against Tailwind 4.3.0 to be certain: a correctly-spelled step emits three properties, this repo’s steps emit one.

.text-probe { font-size: …; line-height: …; letter-spacing: …; }  ← correct suffixes
.text-label { font-size: …; }  ← this repo

A bespoke token has never carried anything but size. That is why hand-typing text-[11px] costs nothing, and why components carry 190 hand-applied leading-* and 229 tracking-* classes putting back what the ramp dropped. Left is what ships today; right is the same markup once the suffixes are correct.

Label tracks at 0.08em and sets solid. Display stacks at 1. Body at 1.6. No component asked for any of it.

The cost of repairing: the 190 leading-* hand-patches now compete with a token that finally asserts itself. Most will agree; the ones that don’t will move. A full VR run (~40 min) and a real diff, pre-go-live.

The cost of ratifying (delete the 11 dead sub-tokens): one commit, zero pixels moved — and “the ramp” permanently means “a list of font sizes”, with leading and tracking hand-carried forever.

3 · Q3 — how small does a label need to be?

Your worry: collapsing to 11px flattens the hierarchy, because a 12px value with an 11px label above it is no contrast at all. Two things make that narrower than it looks. Body copy here is 14–16px, not 12px — text-[12px] is 13 sites in the whole app. And the label is mono, uppercase, tracked: the register separates it, not the point size.

The arithmetic, if you want it

Pairing Ratio Reads as

The crowding you feared is real, but it sits at 11 → 12, not 11 → 14. mono-sm has 13 uses. Retire that step and the bottom of the ramp becomes 11 → 14 → 16 → 18, which separates on its own without a sub-floor label.
