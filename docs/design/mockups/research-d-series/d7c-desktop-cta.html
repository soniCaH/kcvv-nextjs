What happens to the CTA?

The D7a and D7b frames rendered the mobile ledger rows only. MatchStrip has a second layout above lg: a two-slide switch with prev/next arrows and a Wedstrijddetails button. All of it assumes a cream ground.

Everything ink-coloured in that row breaks on #133d28

The desktop half is built from cream-ground assumptions:

  • the CTA is getButtonClasses({ variant: "primary" }) — bg-jersey-deep text-cream, border-2 border-ink, shadow-paper-sm (ink)
  • the arrows are border-ink text-ink hover:bg-cream-soft
  • the dividers are border-ink/15, the slide label text-ink-muted
  • the team names are text-ink

The shipped answer already exists and needs no new variant. Button's inverted is defined as bg-cream text-ink with shadow-paper-sm-soft — and --shadow-paper-sm-soft exists, in its own words, "wherever the standard --shadow-paper-sm (ink) would vanish … any chrome surface on a dark / ink panel". This is that case exactly.

This does not change D7b's answer, it changes its size. "The strip takes a dark ground on match day" is not one class on the <aside> — it is a dark-ground variant of both layouts: CTA primary → inverted, arrows and dividers to cream alphas, team names and score to cream, the slide label to a cream alpha, and the meta line to warm. Roughly a dozen class swaps behind one boolean, against a locked component — so it wants its own checkpoint against matchstrip-locked.md, and a Storybook story per ground rather than a quick edit.
