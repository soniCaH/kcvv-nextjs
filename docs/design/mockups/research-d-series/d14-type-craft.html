How to read that test

If the 500 row is visibly heavier than 400 and lighter than 700, the weight is in the kit and Y5 is buildable. If 500 looks identical to 400, the browser fell back and Y5 is dead until the Typekit kit is changed — which is a kit-configuration task, not a design one.

One rule governs the whole D14 group. Y1, Y8 and Y2 are ramp changes — they go in @theme as steps, never as hand-applied leading-* or tracking-* utilities. That is the exact drift #2417 repaired, and the reason four parallel type systems existed before it.
