#2429 · Filter and tab chrome

Overflow: what a filter row does when it outgrows the screen

Three candidates, at a real 375 px phone width, with the real /kalender facet set — the site's worst case at six chips, one of them Supportersactiviteit. Every row is live: press a chip and the surface below filters for real.

Locked in this ticket so far —

1 · One primitive, colour as a prop. FilterTabs gains a per-facet fill; EventFilterBar and KalenderFilterBar become configs of it.

2 · Chips narrow, segments switch. Detached chips mean "this can remove things"; a joined segmented control means "this swaps the view, nothing disappears".

3 · The Alles chip is the only reset. No second control in the empty state — which is what makes overflow load-bearing rather than cosmetic.

Chip size is still open — every candidate below uses today's FilterTabs md (11 px label, 8 px vertical padding, ~31 px tall). The site currently ships four different heights: 24, 27, 31 and 44 px.
