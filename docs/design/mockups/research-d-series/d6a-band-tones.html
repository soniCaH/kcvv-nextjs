Design decision pass · D6a · C5 placement

Where does the band tone land?

C is agreed: colour maps to Senioren / Bovenbouw / Middenbouw / Onderbouw, derived free from getYouthDivision(). What is still open is how many elements it touches — and that decides how much semantic pressure the tones are under.

The inconsistency worth resolving now

D5 rejected reusing alert-soft / warning-soft as section grounds because a band in "this went well" green sitting under an Alert in the same green is a collision. The four tones here include --color-alert and --color-warning, which is the same reuse.

The defence is scale and adjacency: an 11px kicker is not a full-bleed ground, and no Alert ever renders next to a team card. That defence gets weaker the more of the page the tone occupies — which is exactly what this question sets.

Gewestelijk

Belt and braces. Strongest colour identity, and the version where a team card taken out of context — a search result, a related-teams strip — still carries its band.

The out-of-context argument is the only real case for the kicker. Inside /ploegen the group heading is always visible, so a per-card tone is redundant. Elsewhere — a search hit, a related-team strip, a card lifted onto the homepage — there is no heading, and the tone would be the only thing saying which band it belongs to. Whether that matters depends on whether those surfaces exist, and today they mostly do not.
