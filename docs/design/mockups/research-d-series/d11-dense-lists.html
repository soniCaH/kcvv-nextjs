Where a chip wall is safe, and where it is not

Sponsors is not a candidate. Sponsor logos are the content — greyscale→hover is a locked treatment and the logo is what a sponsor is paying for. A wall of sponsor names in 10px mono deletes the deliverable.

Players are a candidate with a caveat: 0 of 294 have a slug, so the chips link nowhere today. A wall of unlinked names is still a better squad list than a wall of monograms — but it means the chip must be styled as a label, not as a button, or it promises a click it cannot honour.

Two constraints S1 inherits

#2598. It adds width beside display type in a hero — the exact region whose overflow root cause is unresolved. Not to be prototyped against a live page until it is.

PRODUCT.md's data rule. "Fields the upstream does not reliably provide — venue on a match, some competition metadata, is_home on match detail — must never be designed into a layout as guaranteed." A four-row spec table with an empty Terrein row is a worse hero than one mono line. The rows have to be built from what is present, not from a fixed template.

S5 is the one with a decision already depending on it. D8 approved /inhoud, and a contents page without leader dots is just a list — so if S5 is rejected, /inhoud ships in the plain row style shown on the left above.
