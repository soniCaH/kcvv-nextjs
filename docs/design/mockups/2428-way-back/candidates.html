The up-link.

#2428 settled the rule: every route whose breadcrumb trail is deeper than Home → self gets one link to its structural parent, labelled with the bare parent name. 17 routes. It deliberately did not settle what that link looks like or where it sits. Eight treatments below, rendered against the openings they actually have to survive.

Resolved 2026-08-10 — variant 8, the paper chip

  • Treatment: variant 8. An object, not a line — a bordered, offset-shadowed chip carrying the canonical press-down. 30px target from its own padding, no dependency on the type ramp.
  • Dark bands: inside, tone-swapped. The 4 dark routes render the chip within the band (cream border, warm shadow) so the band keeps its flush arrival. Everywhere else the page renders it above the opening.
  • Alignment: always the container's left edge. One rule for all 17. EventHero is the only centred opening and it tolerates a left-aligned chip rather than the chip adapting per route.

Everything below is the working that got there — kept because the rejected variants carry the reasons.

Chart correction — the two "full-bleed dark bands" are cream

The ticket's Notes name MatchHero and TeamHero as the full-bleed dark-band stress cases. Both are wrong:

  • MatchHero is a <TapedCard bg="cream"> rendered inside <PageContainer className="py-12"> (wedstrijd/[matchId]/page.tsx).
  • TeamHero is a plain cream <section> inside <PageContainer> (ploegen/[slug]/page.tsx:201).
  • PlayerHero and StaffHero are cream <section>s too.

The real full-bleed dark bands among the 17 are UltrasHero (/club/ultras) and BoardHero (/club/bestuur, /club/jeugdbestuur, /club/angels) — both <header class="bg-jersey-deep-dark"> starting flush against the sticky site header, with no cream above them. That is a harder case than the ticket described, because there is nowhere on cream to put a link. Four routes, not two.

But cost it against #2426's build, not today's tree. #2426 deletes BoardHero: the three board pages move onto PageHero register="dark", and the 8 hand-rolled routes onto PageHero register="minimal". Post-#2426, 9 of the 17 up-link routes render their opening through PageHero (/club/[slug], /club/contact, /tegenstander/[clubId], /club/geschiedenis, /club/word-lid, /galerij/[slug], /ploegen/[slug]/wedstrijden + the 3 board pages), and the dark register is PageHero ×3 + UltrasHero ×1 — two components, not the eight #2428 costed a slot against.

Second correction: EventHero is text-center inside a 680 column — so it is the alignment outlier, which the Notes never mention.

Constraints carried in from #2428 / #2426 / #2394

  1. One link, one parent, bare parent name. Never Terug naar …, never Alle …, never a chain, never referrer-derived.
  2. CaretLeft exists in icons.redesign.ts; ArrowLeft does not.
  3. The kicker slot carries domain data on 8 of 10 detail routes. Verified in source: MatchHero → match status, EventHero → date range, PlayerHero → team label, TeamHero → "KCVV Elewijt · Jeugd", EditorialHero → type + date.
  4. No hover-only affordances. Visibly a link at rest or it fails.
  5. 24px minimum target. --text-label is 0.6875rem with --text-label--lh: 1 — an 11px line box, so every variant must say how it reaches 24.
  6. Sharp corners, press-down hover, jersey-deep not bright jersey, Phosphor Fill icons.

Red dashed outlines mark the hit target wherever a specimen carries the showtarget class.

The three questions the render surfaced

  1. Is the dark band an exception or a second rule? Four routes (/club/ultras + three createBoardPage leaves) have an opening that starts flush against the sticky header. Either the page renders the up-link above the opening and those four render it inside (two component changes, one written exception), or the affordance moves into the chrome (variant 6) and there is no exception at all.
  2. Line or object? Variant 1 is quieter and cheaper; variant 8 is unmistakably a control at rest, reaches 24px without argument, and is tone-portable — but it is louder than a link on a page whose other bordered-and-shadowed things are buttons that do rather than go.
  3. Does the link follow the container or the opening? EventHero is centred; MatchHero sits inside a taped card; /wedstrijd and /spelers already put a full-bleed MatchStripSlot in this exact position. One fixed alignment across 17 routes, or per-route adaptation?
