Does match day get its own register?

§5B proposes five elements that switch on together on the day of a match and vanish otherwise. D1 already killed one of the five (the crest plate). Before the aesthetics, the hour-by-hour table — because this is behaviour over time, and an option list cannot show a band that is stuck on at 3 a.m.

Four facts, checked against the code, that shrink the proposal

  1. There is no "in progress" state — anywhere. MATCH_STATUS_VALUES in packages/api-contract/src/schemas/match.ts is scheduled · finished · forfeited · postponed · cancelled · stopped. A match being played right now is scheduled. Nothing in the data can say "live". So §5B switch 3 (marching ants while in progress) and switch 5 (the flickering live dot) have no source.
  2. PSD flips straight from scheduled to finished the moment a goal exists. The mapping is literally 0 (no goals) → scheduled, 0 (has goals) → finished. If any score reaches PSD before the whistle, the site says finished 1–0 during the second half.
  3. PRODUCT.md:56 forbids live in-play scores outright. A ● LIVE marker promises coverage the product has decided not to have.
  4. The homepage is revalidate = 900 — apps/web/src/app/(landing)/page.tsx:493. Anything clock-derived is up to 15 minutes wrong in both directions, and a page cached mid-match serves that state to everyone until it regenerates.

There is also a duplication: MatchStrip already sits on the landing page with the last result and the next fixture. A wire strip under the header would be a second piece of match chrome, above the first.

What survives of §5B under each answer. Under B: the wire strip, date-derived, no dot and no ants — and the marquee becomes optional, since it says the same thing louder. Under C: everything, plus a client clock that can only ever remove the band and never add it, plus a shortened revalidate on the homepage, plus a rule for what happens when the status flips to finished mid-match. Under A: §5B closes, and M2, M3 and M10 close with it, since all three exist only inside this mode. Anything here that touches the fixture row is gated on #2599 regardless.
