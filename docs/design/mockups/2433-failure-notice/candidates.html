How a failure notice reads inside a section

#2433 rule 3 says a failed section keeps the page and says so. This page decides the form of that sentence. The copy is locked by rule 9, the absence of an action by rule 8, and the absence of a colour of its own by rule 11.

Resolved 2026-08-11. (b) inline accent, mechanism b1 — serif italic + accent colour (font-display italic + jersey-deep; warm on dark), accent on the failure words. The notice restores its TeamSectionNav chip. It is not a new primitive — it is #2427's <EmptyState> Tier 2 carrying different copy, so the build sequences after #2427. Frame on cream: border-2 border-dashed border-ink/30.

The ticket's chart is wrong, in the way this map's charts keep being wrong. It asks how a notice reads "inside a section that already has a heading", and says the cream case is the unsolved one.

Measured against the tree: #2433 puts a notice on exactly three sections across two routes — and all three are cream. The dark case has zero in-scope instances; the only dark one (FirstTeamsBlock) is the homepage, which is #2402's and out of scope here. Cream is not the unsolved case — it is the only case.

And two of the three have no heading at all. On /ploegen/[slug] the klassement is a bare <section aria-label="Klassement"> and the wedstrijden section carries only an inline Eerstvolgende label. The double-heading fear is real on one site, not three. The unsolved problem is the opposite one: what names the section at all, when the section is the thing that failed.

Fourth fact the Notes miss: showStandings/showMatches (page.tsx:163–178) gate the sticky TeamSectionNav item as well as the section. So a notice is not a purely visual change — it decides whether the nav grows an entry pointing at a notice.

Chosen: (b) inline accent · now pick its mechanism

What an accent inside body copy actually is

There is no body-copy accent on this site to copy. EditorialHeading's emphasis is display-type-only, and every italic in src/components arrives with a switch to font-display. So (b) has to invent its mechanism — these are the four the existing vocabulary can reach. All render the same locked sentence on the real cream site.

b1 · serif italic + accent colour the site's only italic

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

Literally EditorialHeading's emphasis, moved into a <p>. Unmistakably the house accent — and unmistakably a serif island, at a different x-height, mid-sentence.

b2 · accent colour only no font switch

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

Stays in font-body, so the line keeps one texture. But green is this site's link colour — coloured words inside a sentence read as a link that does not click.

b3 · weight only no colour at all

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

The only candidate that cannot argue with rule 11 (no colour of its own) and needs no per-surface tone. Also the quietest — arguably too quiet to count as "the editorial voice" the owner asked for.

b4 · highlighter sweep the marker variant

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

<HighlighterStroke> already works on any children and clones per wrapped line. But the marker is the site's celebratory emphasis — using it to highlight an outage is the loudest possible reading of a note.

And a second axis the ticket never named

Which words carry the accent

#2433's example accents the failure. But the chart correction found that on two of three sites the sentence is also the section's only label — which makes accenting the subject a real alternative, because it strengthens the labelling rather than the bad news.

target: the failure#2433's

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

Uitslagen en wedstrijden zijn even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

The emphasis is on the words that mean broken, so the loudest thing in the band is the bad news.

target: the subjectdiscovered

Het klassement is even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

Uitslagen en wedstrijden zijn even niet beschikbaar. Probeer het later opnieuw.

The emphasis names the missing thing, which is the job the sentence is already doing alone on the two anonymous sections. Reads as a note about the klassement rather than an announcement that something is broken.

Secondary — decide only if the frame survives

The dashed frame has never been drawn on cream

HELD_OPEN_FRAME is border-cream/40 against jersey-deep-dark — roughly a 40 % veil over a dark ground. There is no agreed cream equivalent, and the site's existing dashed borders on cream use three different values.
