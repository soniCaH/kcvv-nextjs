Cream on green.
How dark does the green need to go?

Four homepage surfaces pair cream #f5f1e6 on jersey-deep #008755. Judge them by eye — scroll, don't read the numbers first.

Your two constraints:

1 — No WCAG target is adopted. Readable is the test, the ratios are only a tiebreaker.

2 — Cream stays. White is a fallback you'd rather not take, and never a background.

If the headings render in Georgia rather than Freight, Typekit didn't authorise this origin — the colour judgement is unaffected.

What the numbers say that the eye might miss: the /90 opacity on the youth paragraph costs more than a whole step of green — 4.05 → 3.56. Dropping it is free and changes no token.

Row D is highlighted because #007c46 already exists as jersey-link. Choosing it merges two greens instead of adding a sixth, and rewrites the Two-Greens Rule into something simpler: one green for decoration, one green for anything carrying text.
