The imageless card is not a hypothetical — it ships today, and the site already owns three answers it never asks for.

The ticket asks what a card looks like with a crest instead of a photo. Measuring first changed the question. The imageless card is already in production on /nieuws/[slug]: mapBffRelatedItems hardcodes imageUrl: null for every page item, so every PAGINA card in the related row renders the fallback. 2 of 6 sampled articles show one right now.

And the fallback pattern is not homeless. The ticket says it lives in MatchArticleLinkCard, which #2443 deletes. It also lives in NewsCard.tsx:268-278 — byte-identical, same repeating-linear-gradient string. Deleting the match card orphans nothing. (It is also 135°, not the 45° the ticket states.)

The real finding: this site has three subject-specific no-photo artefacts, all locked and shipping — and NewsCard knows none of them. A player is a jersey figure on /spelers/[slug] and in the squad grid, but a hatch in the related row. Same player, same missing photo, two answers.

Candidate B · wire what already exists

The card asks its subject for its artefact

Person → <JerseyIllustration>. Team → <JerseyShirt> + its letter. Club → its crest, contained on paper, never cover-cropped. Article / page → the hatch stays, because there is no artefact for "a piece of writing". Four primitives, all shipping; nothing new is drawn.

The crest is the case the ticket worried about, and the measurement dissolves it: a contained crest in a 288px card's 16:9 slot lands at ~121px tall (284px inner width → 160px slot height → 76% cap) — well under its 180px native size. No upscale at all, as long as it is contained rather than covered.
