Two corrections to yesterday's page

There is no speckle on the paper today. --pattern-paper-grain is wired into exactly one rule — .taped-figure::after at 4% multiply — plus SubjectAvatar. It is a photo treatment. The cream ground is flat. So T3 is not "replace the grain", it is "give the paper a texture for the first time", and the speckle layer shown here at 5% is itself new.

Black at 9% opacity is grey. That is why A reads as dirt rather than as stock — on a palette that deleted its last neutral in #2342, any texture drawn in ink introduces the one layer the system does not have. B, C and D draw the identical noise in --color-cream-deep instead, as a mask. Same maths, opposite result: the paper varies in paper tones.

Whatever wins is two custom properties and one rule on body. If none of the four is right, the honest answer is that the paper stays flat and T3 closes; a texture nobody is happy with is worse than none.
