Q3 — does the squad chip survive at 26px?

#2516 already decided the family: the chip on /ploegen/[slug] is a tag, so it moves from font-display-big to mono and drops tabular-nums. What #2516 could not judge — because the datum renders zero times — is whether a 26 × 26 disc still centres once real digits are in it. The site's default figure set is oldstyle, so 3 4 5 7 9 carry descenders and 6 8 ascend: in a fixed square they sit low.

Mono, centred — #2516 as written
7 8

IBM Plex Mono's figures are lining and monospaced by construction — no descenders, so the disc centres. The oldstyle worry the ticket raised belongs to the Freight faces, not to mono.

Display-big, centred — today's code
7 8

The state the ticket described: Freight's oldstyle 7 drops below the optical centre of the square. This is what #2516 is already removing.

Mono + optical nudge
7 8

A 2px nudge on top of mono. Included to be falsified: if mono already centres, this is a magic number with nothing to fix, and the map's rule is that a departure needs a reason.

Resolved — 2026-08-12

What was decided.

  1. Candidate B, the reserved slot. One hero shape for every player. The held-open slot on the not-yet-typed players is the accepted cost, and it is why the build sequences after the content pass.
  2. The number is a declared editorial fact, for all 294. Typed by hand from the kit list. No Drupal migration (54% stale), no match-sheet seeding. The field already exists and is already wired — what it lacks is validation and a fill-progress preview.
  3. The match page keeps the sheet's number. The lineup reports what was worn that day; the profile declares the squad number. Two different facts, so they are allowed to disagree — and on 41% of repeat players they do.
  4. Zero is not a number. player.number ? player.number : undefined at the transform, min(1).max(99).integer() at the schema. Not a null-vs-undefined question: ?? never caught 0 either way, and the normalized contract is 42-to-1 on S.optional.
