Design decision pass · D7b · match-day ground

Green is an event. Is this the event?

Same band, same relabel, same slot. Only the ground changes. Each frame is the homepage on a Saturday morning with kickoff at 15:00.

Why green has the best claim here — and why red has the worst

The Rare Green Rule says green is an event, not a surface treatment. Match day is the only genuinely recurring event this site has. If green is ever going to be spent on a state rather than a link, this is the state — roughly thirty Saturdays a year, and nothing else on the site competes for it.

Red already means something, and it is bad news. --color-alert is the dusty brick that Alerts use, and --color-card-red is what MatchStatusBadge paints CANC in. A red band across the top of the homepage on a match morning is the same colour as "afgelast" — the exact message it must not send.
