What does a team-page loading skeleton draw?

One skeleton per route has to serve every team on it. The two extremes below are the same file: a U9 page with 3 nav pills and 6 squad cards, and the A-team with 5 pills and 29 cards. Each panel is a candidate rule, shown as the skeleton and then what it actually resolves into.

Fixed before this question
  • #2540 — the competitive block has four states. A team with no fixtures gets one unheaded status line and no #klassement / #wedstrijden sections and no nav entries. With fixtures, both sections render with their own h2 and seam.
  • The loaded shape moves during the season. Every youth page is 3 pills today (0 fixtures for season 9) and becomes 5 the week the reeksen publish. The skeleton cannot be pinned to one of them.
  • #2541 — a squad that partitions into one group renders with no heading; U9 is one group of 6, the A-team is 4 + 1 + 24.
  • #2602 — youth directory groups are 1 / 4 / 4 / 7, cards 230px desktop.

D

Invariant-only, per route

Same discipline as B, but the invariant is computed per route rather than assumed to be the hero: each skeleton draws its own route's fixed opening at full fidelity, then one content field. For [slug] that is hero + nav strip; for wedstrijden the editorial header; for /ploegen the header plus the two flagship blocks, which are the only genuinely fixed count on the surface (always exactly two).
